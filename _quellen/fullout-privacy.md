# Privacy Policy for the FullOut App

Last updated: 17 November 2026 · [Deutsche Fassung](/fullout/datenschutz/)

This is a translation. The [German version](/fullout/datenschutz/) is legally binding.

## 1. Controller

DoLabst – owner and postal address: see [Legal notice](/impressum/)
Email: fullout@dolabst.de

We do not have a data protection officer; we are not required to appoint one.

## 2. Summary

- FullOut works **without an account** and without a server of ours.
- **The app does not send any data to us.** Everything you save in the app (times, player names, settings) stays on your device.
- **The camera does not take photos or record videos.** The image is only analysed live on the device.
- No tracking, no advertising, no analytics, no third-party SDKs.

## 3. No Data Transmission to Us

We do not operate any servers for FullOut and there is no user account. The app does not connect to us over the internet. We therefore do not receive any personal data from the app.

The app contains no analytics, tracking or advertising services and no third-party software components (SDKs) that collect data about you.

## 4. Camera (Front Camera)

In **camera mode**, FullOut measures time using the front camera: the app detects whether the "Sensor Zone" (the area of the front camera) is covered. To do this, it analyses the camera image **only live on your device**, e.g. brightness and coverage.

- **No photos or videos are taken, stored or transmitted.**
- The camera is only used when you use camera mode and have granted camera permission in iOS. Using it is voluntary.
- Without the camera, you can use **finger mode**.
- You can revoke the permission at any time in the iOS settings (Settings → FullOut → Camera). Camera mode is then no longer available.

**Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the app function you have chosen).

## 5. Data Stored Only on Your Device

The app stores the following locally on your device:

- measured times with date
- optional player names (freely chosen, nicknames are fine)
- settings (sound on/off, camera or finger mode, whether the introduction has been shown)
- whether "FullOut Pro" is unlocked

This data is not transmitted to us or to third parties. You can delete it in the app ("Delete times" or delete individual runs) or remove it completely by deleting the app.

**Storage period:** until you delete the data in the app or delete the app.

**Note on backups:** If you use iCloud or device backups, this data may become part of your own backup. We have no access to it.

**Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

## 6. Sharing a Result

When you share a result, the app creates an image on your device showing your time (and, if applicable, name and rank). Only when you tap "Share" is this image passed via the iOS share sheet to the app you choose (e.g. a messenger). From then on, the privacy policy of that app or service applies. We receive nothing.

## 7. Sound and Vibration

FullOut plays sounds and vibrations as feedback. This happens only locally on the device, requires no permission and no data is collected.

## 8. In-App Purchase "FullOut Pro"

FullOut Pro is a **one-time purchase, not a subscription**. Purchases and "Restore Purchases" are handled exclusively by Apple (App Store).

- We receive **no payment information** and **not your Apple ID**.
- The app only learns from Apple whether Pro has been purchased.

**Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. Apple privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

## 9. Processing by Apple

When you download the app and make purchases, Apple processes data under its own responsibility, e.g. your Apple ID and download information. If you have enabled "Share with App Developers" in iOS (Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements), Apple also provides us with anonymised crash and usage data. We have no influence over this. More information: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

## 10. Hosting of This Website (GitHub Pages)

This website (dolabst.de) is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub B.V., Netherlands / GitHub, Inc., USA). When you visit it, GitHub processes your IP address and technical data to deliver the page and protect it against abuse. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. GitHub privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

## 11. Your Rights

You have the right to
- access your stored data (Art. 15 GDPR),
- rectification (Art. 16 GDPR),
- erasure (Art. 17 GDPR),
- restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR),
- data portability (Art. 20 GDPR),
- object (Art. 21 GDPR).

Since we do not receive any data from the app, we are generally unable to provide information about individual users' data. You are still welcome to contact us at fullout@dolabst.de.

## 12. Right to Lodge a Complaint

State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information North Rhine-Westphalia (LDI NRW), https://www.ldi.nrw.de
