Privacy Policy for the RIVET App

Last updated: November 2026 · Deutsche Fassung

1. Controller

DoLabst – owner and postal address: see Legal notice
Email: rivet@dolabst.de

We do not have a data protection officer; we are not required to appoint one.

2. Summary

  • RIVET works without an account and without a server of ours.
  • The app does not send any data over the internet – not to us and not to third parties.
  • Everything you create in the app (players, photos, sounds, scores) stays only on your device.
  • No tracking, no advertising, no analytics, no third-party services.

3. Data that stays on your device

The app stores the following data locally on your device:

  • player names
  • photos (for players and the game)
  • your own sound recordings
  • leaderboard and game scores
  • settings (e.g. sound on/off)

This data is not transmitted to us or to third parties. You can delete it in the app at any time; all of it is removed when you delete the app.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the app's functions)

4. Permissions on your device

The app only asks for access when you use the corresponding feature:

PermissionPurposeWhat happens to the data
CameraTaking photos for players and the gameThe photo is stored only in the app on your device
MicrophoneRecording your own soundsThe recording is stored only in the app on your device
Photo libraryChoosing existing pictures for players and the gameOnly the picture you choose is copied into the app; RIVET does not save anything to your photo library

You can revoke any permission in your device's settings. The affected feature is then no longer available; the rest of the game keeps working.

5. In-app purchase

RIVET offers an optional one-time purchase (Premium).

  • The purchase is processed exclusively by Apple via the App Store.
  • We do not receive or store any payment information.
  • "Restore purchases" is also handled by Apple: the app only asks the App Store which purchases belong to your Apple Account.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR

6. App Store

When you download the app or make a purchase, Apple processes data (e.g. your Apple Account, payment data, device information) under its own responsibility. We have no influence on this. Privacy at Apple: https://www.apple.com/privacy/

7. No tracking, no advertising

The app does not use any analytics, tracking or advertising services and does not contain any third-party services that process personal data.

8. Hosting of this website (GitHub Pages)

This website (dolabst.de) is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub B.V., Netherlands / GitHub, Inc., USA). When you visit it, GitHub processes your IP address and technical data to deliver the page and protect it against abuse. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Privacy at GitHub: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

9. Children

The app is not specifically directed at children under 16. Since the app does not send any data to us, we do not collect data from children either.

10. Your rights

You have the right to

  • access your stored data (Art. 15 GDPR),
  • rectification (Art. 16 GDPR),
  • erasure (Art. 17 GDPR),
  • restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR),
  • data portability (Art. 20 GDPR),
  • object to processing (Art. 21 GDPR),
  • withdraw consent with effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR), e.g. device permissions.

Since we do not store any personal data of users, we usually cannot provide information about specific persons. Just write to us at rivet@dolabst.de.

11. Right to lodge a complaint

You can lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, e.g. the one responsible for us:
State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information North Rhine-Westphalia, https://www.ldi.nrw.de

12. Changes

If the app or the legal requirements change, we will update this policy. The version published here applies.
