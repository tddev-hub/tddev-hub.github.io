2. General Information

Protecting your personal data is important to us. Our apps can generally be used without transmitting personal data to us.

We process personal data only in accordance with the applicable legal provisions, in particular the GDPR.

3. Data Processing in the Apps

3.1 No Data Transmission to Us

We do not collect, store, or process personal data on our own servers. Data created and used within the apps remains exclusively on your device.

3.2 Local Storage

The apps may store the following data locally on your device:

  • Names
  • Photos
  • Audio recordings

This data:

  • is stored exclusively on your device,
  • is not transmitted to us or to third parties, and
  • can be deleted by you at any time.

Storage period: Data remains stored until you delete it or uninstall the app.

3.3 Device Permissions

The apps may request access to the following device features where required for app functionality:

  • Camera
  • Microphone
  • Photo library

These permissions are used solely to provide app features such as capturing photos, recording audio, or selecting and saving images.

No data is transmitted to us or to third parties through the use of these permissions.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of the app's functions)

When you download the apps via the Apple App Store or make in-app purchases, Apple may process personal data independently, such as your Apple ID, payment information, and device-related information.

We have no influence over data processing carried out by Apple.

More information: https://www.apple.com/privacy/
